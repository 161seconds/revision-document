One round, four moments.

What your child sees, from the first page to the last.

  1. Read a page

    One short sentence at a time, with the word being read lit up.

  2. Hear a word

    Stuck on a word? Tap it and Sprout says it out loud.

  3. Grow a plant

    Every page they read helps today’s plant along.

  4. Stop at five

    The round ends itself at five minutes, before anyone gets tired.

Watch the garden fill.

Plants stay put between rounds, so there is always a reason to come back.
