How a frame starts

  1. You send the scheme

    Plans, sections or a model, at whatever stage it has reached.

  2. We read it and call

    An engineer, not an office manager, goes through it with you.

  3. We propose the frame

    A grid, the options for it, and what each asks of the plan.
