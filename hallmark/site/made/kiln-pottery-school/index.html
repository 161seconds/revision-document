  • The six-week course

    One weeknight evening a week, the same wheel each time.

  • A Saturday taster

    One session at the wheel to find out if it is for you.

  • The room

    Wheels, glaze buckets, bisque on the shelves and a kiln at the back.

1. Centre the clay

Weeks one and two.

Wedge
Knead the air out so the clay runs true.
Centre
Brace your elbows and press until the lump stops wobbling.
Open
Thumb down, then out, to make a floor.

2. Pull the walls

Weeks three and four: cylinders, then bowls, then a mug with a handle.

3. Trim, glaze, fire

Weeks five and six, and two trips through the kiln.

  1. Trim

    Week five: cut a foot.

  2. Bisque

    The first firing.

  3. Glaze

    Week six: dip, pour or brush.

  4. Collect

    Once the kiln has cooled.
