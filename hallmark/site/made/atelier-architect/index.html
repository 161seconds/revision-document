What do you design?

Mostly homes, for the people who will live in them.

New houses

On infill plots, garden sites and open land.

Extensions

  • Kitchens that open to the garden
  • Studies and garden rooms
  • Loft rooms and new stairs

Refits

  • Whole-house plans, reworked
  • New openings and daylight
  • Insulation, windows and heat

Old buildings

Barns, terraces and older houses, repaired and reused.

How does a project run?

Each stage is agreed and priced before it begins.

NumberStageWhat happensYou receive
1BriefWe visit the site and listenA written brief and a survey
2ConceptTwo or three options, drawnPlans, sketches and a model
3PlanningWe prepare the applicationThe drawings the council asks for
4TechnicalDetail for builders to priceA tender set
5On siteVisits through the buildSign-off at handover

What should it feel like inside?

Calm rooms, plain materials, daylight from more than one side.

What else do people ask?

Is my project too small?

Rarely: a single room is as welcome as a whole house.

How are fees worked out?

A fixed fee for each stage, agreed in writing before it starts.

Will I need planning permission?

Smaller work often does not; we check at the first visit.

Can I use my own builder?

Yes, and we stay on hand through the build.

Where is your project?
