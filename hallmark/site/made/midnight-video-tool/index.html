Before you send the take

Does it post for us?

No. It exports finished files, and you post them where you already do.

Can we move a cut?

Every cut is yours. Move it by the word, or mark a moment it missed.

What about two cameras?

Drop in both angles and Reelcut cuts to whoever is speaking.

Who keeps the footage?

You do. Delete a project and its files go with it.

Send us your longest interview

The clips come back by email.
