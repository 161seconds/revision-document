In your words, then ours

“It clicks when I pedal.”

Usually a pedal, a crank or the chain.

Pedals
Threads tight, bearings turning smoothly.
Crank bolts
Torqued so the arms stop creaking.
Chain
Cleaned, oiled and measured for wear.
Bottom bracket
Checked for play and grinding.

“The brakes squeal, or feel soft.”

Pads, cables and what they grip.

Pads
Worn or glazed pads swapped out.
Cables or hoses
Tension set, or the hydraulics bled.
Rims and rotors
Cleaned and trued so pads bite evenly.
Levers
Firm well before they reach the bar.

“The gears skip.”

Cable tension, the hanger, a worn chain.

Indexing
Each click lands on one cog.
Hanger
Straightened if a knock bent it.
Chain and cassette
Measured, since a worn pair slips.
Gear cables
Frayed or sticky cables replaced.

“It’s flat again.”

Whatever keeps letting the air out.

Tyre
Searched for glass, thorns and wire.
Rim tape
Covering every spoke hole.
Tube and valve
Patched or replaced, valve checked.
Pressure
Set for you and the tyre.

“It hasn’t been ridden in years.”

A full once-over before the first ride.

Tyres
Perished rubber and tubes replaced.
Brakes
Seized parts freed, pads set.
Chain and gears
Rust off, every gear shifting.
Bolts
Every one checked and tightened.

Quiet again, at your own door.

No car rack, and no pushing it to a shop and back.
