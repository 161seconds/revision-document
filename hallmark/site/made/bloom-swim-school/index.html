From splashing to swimming lengths

Four stages, each taken at the child’s own pace.

  1. 1

    Face in the water

    Blowing bubbles, ears in, eyes open under the surface.

  2. 2

    Floating

    On the front and the back, with a float, then without.

  3. 3

    Kick and pull

    First strokes across the width of the shallow end.

  4. 4

    A length alone

    Front crawl and backstroke, end to end, unaided.

A teaching pool, kept warm

Warm enough to stay in for the whole lesson, shallow enough to stand.

What parents ask first

Must they swim already?
No. The first stage is getting their face wet.
What should they bring?
A costume, a towel, and goggles if they like them.
What if they’re nervous?
They start where they can stand. Nobody is pushed under.
