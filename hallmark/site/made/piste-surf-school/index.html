Your first morning on a board

  1. Fitted

    A wetsuit and a soft-top board, sized to you.

  2. On the sand

    The pop-up, practised on the sand until it is one move.

  3. In the whitewater

    Waist deep, an instructor beside you picking the waves.

  4. Standing

    Then you ride one in on your own.
