How a river project runs

Slowly, and always with the same people.

  1. Walk it

    From the source to the sea, on foot and by boat.

  2. Stay

    A season with one family, one village, one landing.

  3. Go back

    Every year, to the same doors, until the story turns.

  4. Print

    Silver gelatin, by hand, in her own darkroom.
