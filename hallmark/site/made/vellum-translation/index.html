A translated novel should read as if it were first written in the language you hold it in.

Who reads the book first?

The translator, cover to cover, before a single sentence is changed.

What goes out, what comes back?

From a first read to a finished text.

StageYou sendWe send back
ReadingThe bookA reader’s report for the rights decision
SampleOne chapterThat chapter, translated
TranslationThe full textThe finished book
EditYour editor’s queriesA revised draft

What else do publishers ask?

Do you take poetry or plays?

Rarely. Novels and essays are the work; ask, and we say plainly if a book is not for us.

Proofs or a printed copy?

Either. A PDF of the proofs is easiest to work from and to quote against.

Whose name goes on the book?

The translator’s, on the title page at least, as your contract sets out.

How is the fee set?

By length and by the kind of text. The quote names one figure before work starts.

Which book should we read next?

We reply with a date to read it.
