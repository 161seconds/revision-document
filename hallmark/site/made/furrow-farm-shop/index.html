A week’s food from one Shropshire farm.

Weekly veg boxes, a butcher’s counter and a cafe open at weekends.

Order a veg box Find the shop

1. The field fills the box

Each week’s box holds what came up that week, picked and packed on the farm.

The box through the year

Roughly how the field turns, season by season.

  1. Spring

    Mar to May

    Asparagus, spring greens, radishes, new potatoes

  2. Summer

    Jun to Aug

    Broad beans, courgettes, tomatoes, salad leaves

  3. Autumn

    Sep to Nov

    Squash, beetroot, apples, maincrop potatoes

  4. Winter

    Dec to Feb

    Leeks, kale, parsnips, swede, sprouts
