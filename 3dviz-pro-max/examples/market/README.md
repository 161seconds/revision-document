# The Harvest Counter

A compact market stall with a thick scalloped awning, timber supports, recessed trays, and hand-scale produce.

From `examples/`, run `pnpm install` once, then `pnpm dev` and open `/market/`; `pnpm build` builds all examples.

## Generation prompt

> Use 3dviz-pro-max to create a charming harvest counter whose small-object construction survives close inspection: a striped cloth awning with real thickness and scalloped ends, supported timber posts, recessed produce trays, and fruit at a believable hand scale. Give cloth, wood, metal, and produce distinct surface roles.

## Explore and implementation

Use Overview and Detail, orbit/zoom, and reset. See [scene.js](scene.js) and the [shared market factory](../shared/breadth/src/crafted-architecture.js).

## Assets and limits

The stall and produce are fictional procedural forms with no external assets. It is a visual prop study, not a fabric, structural, food, or ergonomic simulation.

## Skill rules actually applied

The scene uses object-specific silhouette, scale, surface, and support reasoning from [object reasoning](../../skills/3dviz-pro-max/references/object-reasoning.md), [object craft](../../skills/3dviz-pro-max/references/object-craft.md), and [physical interaction](../../skills/3dviz-pro-max/references/physical-interaction.md). Mapping is retrospective.
