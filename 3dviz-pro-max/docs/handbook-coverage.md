# Handbook coverage audit

Initially compared on 2026-09-07 against the maintainer-supplied `3d-art-visualization-and-simulation-handbook-en.md` at 144 recipes and 262 knowledge records. Updated through the Blender/glTF/Three.js craft, scientific and applied-subject collections: current canonical coverage is 223 recipes and 416 knowledge records across the same 17 families. The handbook is a coverage reference, not executable instructions or a source proving scientific claims. Only promoted records count as canonical coverage; source review is separate from runtime validation.

**Finding:** the major taxonomy and all 28 listed style terms are represented. Subject-by-subject coverage is partial. Populating 24 directions does not cover every use case in the handbook, and authored guidance does not establish a tested renderer, solver or host integration.

## Current expansion versus remaining backlog

Earlier completed expansions deepen object archetypes, materials, physical behaviors, tool adapters, inspection, bounded subjects and scientific data boundaries. The first two promoted Blender/Three.js waves add 29 knowledge records: 17 modeling/render/physics handoffs followed by 12 object, surface, decoder/texture-transform and lighting contracts. A third wave adds 21 spatial presentation/composition, motion, interaction and geometry profiles. A fourth wave adds 14 inspection/validation/output/physics records, including conditional XR and animation-pointer transport. Together the four waves add 88 net-new sources and 605 public evidence events. At that checkpoint all 229 authored lexical retrieval cases, all unit tests, schema/evidence, index and package checks passed; the suite has since grown and 241 authored cases passed on 2026-09-08; 209 protected baseline files and the original 414-source prefix remain exact. No Blender scene/export, glTF round trip, decoder, Rapier simulation, Three.js render or visual comparison was executed. Three promoted water-record revisions separately repair a learned coverage/appearance gap.

The accepted subjects close their named slices only. Forced room modes do not establish calibrated reverberation, flat layered seismic rays do not model anisotropic Earth structure, and GHZ correlations do not provide general quantum dynamics. The rejected Yuna experiment contributes no accepted likeness or character-quality result.

The approved collection is complete: 74 knowledge records and 49 recipes were accepted after novelty curation. The original 83-knowledge/51-recipe estimate was flexible, not a quota or remaining queue. Future collection requires a distinct model or missing decision rather than duplicating these bounded slices. Potential extensions include richer continuum/FEA models, broader mathematical and protocol families, multiple-scattering atmosphere models and multi-echelon logistics; these are coverage opportunities, not newly assigned work.

Concrete scientific data, exports, solver executions and XR artifacts still need their own validation. Clinical intervention collection retains its explicit prerequisites. Installation, marketplace, license and showcase work remain separate from dataset completion.

## Audit method and limits

Read the complete handbook and enumerated every live recipe/knowledge title and stable ID, then checked the relevant skill references and generated coverage metadata. Report absence as **no dedicated current record/recipe**, not an inability of an agent to create the result. The skill can combine existing data and conduct task-specific research for an unlisted subject. No numerical percentage is assigned because style profiles, scientific models, user interactions and host integrations have different completion criteria.

The fifth craft wave adds ten style/theme/reasoning profiles, bringing this depth program to 74 records, 89 net-new sources and 655 evidence events. Five overlapping reasoning candidates reuse existing contracts rather than duplicate them. The approved subject collection is now complete as authored and independently reviewed content.

The reviewed mathematics, logic and puzzle subject batch adds twelve recipes, fourteen sources and 92 events. Finite numerical/discrete models retain exact input/state boundaries and explicit Blender-to-Three.js construction; inverse history is distinct from imported-state solving. These are source-reviewed authoring contracts, not executed solvers. The subsequent scientific and applied collections are also complete, as recorded below.
