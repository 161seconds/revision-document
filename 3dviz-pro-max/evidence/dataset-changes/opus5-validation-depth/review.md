# Domain-validation editorial review

The batch contains 72 authored verification procedures across 24 directions. Claude Opus 5 researched and authored the records, then resumed the same session to address independent review of all procedures. Three independent rechecks inspected the corrected emitted data in groups of 24. Twelve residual findings in eleven records were corrected before promotion and accepted in targeted follow-up checks. The final author output remained unchanged during those reviews; exact content hashes identify the promoted revisions.

Corrections preserve applicability, numeric regimes and units, model-versus-display state, explicit rejection/clamping policies, exact lattice identities, graph tie policies, finite numeric inputs and path costs, and temporal/physical boundary cases. Procedures were checked for internal consistency and counterexamples; referenced sources support their bounded factual claims.

These are proposed procedures, not executed visual tests. No renderer, simulation, artifact export or host installation ran for these records. Editorial review and structural/evidence validation passed within the recorded scopes; neither is scientific certification or a runtime pass. Source events preserve the author's actual reads and limitations.
