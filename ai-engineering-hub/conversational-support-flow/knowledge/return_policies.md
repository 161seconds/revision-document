# Return & Refund Policy

This document covers return windows by product category, how to start a
return, refund timing, exchanges, and a few special cases. It's the source
the RETURN_POLICY route retrieves from with RAG — real answers should always
cite the specific section that applies.

## Electronics

Electronics can be returned within 15 days of delivery, unopened and in
original packaging with all accessories and manuals included. Opened
electronics can only be returned if damaged or defective. Damaged or
defective electronics are refunded in full, including original shipping
costs, regardless of the 15-day window. A 10% restocking fee applies to
opened-but-undamaged electronics returned within the window (e.g. a customer
who changes their mind after unboxing).

## Apparel

Apparel can be returned within 30 days of delivery, unworn, unwashed, and
with original tags attached. Swimwear and undergarments can only be returned
if the hygiene liner is intact. Damaged or defective apparel is refunded in
full, including original shipping costs, with no condition requirements.

## Footwear

Footwear can be returned within 30 days of delivery if unworn outdoors (worn
briefly indoors to check fit is fine) and returned in the original box.
Damaged or defective footwear is refunded in full regardless of wear.

## Home & Kitchen

Home and kitchen items can be returned within 30 days of delivery, unused
and in original packaging. Items that came in sealed packaging for hygiene
or safety reasons (e.g. cookware with food-safe coatings) can only be
returned unopened. Damaged or defective items are refunded in full.

## Beauty & Personal Care

For hygiene reasons, beauty and personal care items can only be returned
unopened and in original sealed packaging, within 15 days of delivery.
Opened items are not eligible for return unless damaged or defective on
arrival, in which case they're refunded in full.

## Groceries & Perishables

Grocery and perishable items are final sale and not eligible for return,
except in the case of a damaged shipment or an item that arrived spoiled —
contact support within 48 hours of delivery for a full refund on those.

## Gift Cards

Gift cards are non-returnable and non-refundable once issued.

## Default (Uncategorized Items)

For product categories not covered above, returns are accepted within 30
days of delivery, unused and in original packaging. Damaged or defective
items are refunded in full, including original shipping costs.

## How to Start a Return

Returns are started from the order's tracking page, or by asking support
for the order in question. A prepaid return label is generated automatically
for eligible returns; the customer is not charged return shipping unless a
restocking fee applies (see Electronics above).

## Refund Timing & Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3-5 business days
of the returned item being scanned by the carrier (not after it arrives at
the warehouse — the refund clock starts at drop-off). Store credit refunds,
when chosen instead of the original payment method, post within 24 hours.

## Exchanges

Direct exchanges (same item, different size or color) are available for
Apparel and Footwear within the same return window as a standard return, and
ship as soon as the original item is scanned by the carrier — the customer
doesn't need to wait for the return to be received first.

## Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

Any item that arrives damaged, defective, or different from what was
ordered is refunded in full, including original shipping, regardless of
category or how much time has passed since delivery, as long as it's
reported within a reasonable time of noticing the issue.

## Holiday Extended Returns

Orders placed between November 1 and December 24 get an extended return
window through January 31 of the following year, on top of whatever window
normally applies to that category.

## International Orders

International orders follow the same category windows above, but return
shipping is the customer's responsibility unless the item was damaged,
defective, or incorrect.
